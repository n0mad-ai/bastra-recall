# Supersession ordering correction (#1206), 2026-10-17

New paired measurements; the registrations and previously registered results from #1205 are unchanged.

## Implementation and scope

After fusion and all existing score multipliers, cap a predecessor's score at its retrieved successor's score minus one score-precision unit (0.001), never increasing a score or making it negative. Re-sort before top-k and candidate-pool emission. Resolve chains from the newest candidate backwards. This avoids an arbitrary global penalty that might still leave a high-scoring old version ahead, and preserves the scores of successors and unrelated candidates. BM25, hybrid and BM25 fallback share this gateway.

Single-query recall only compares candidates already retrieved in the same direct/hop lane. No new candidates, cross-filter lookups, disk reads, model calls, settings or schema changes. A targeted query matching only the predecessor remains unchanged. A successor outside the candidate pool, including a missing or filtered successor, causes no demotion. Missing intermediate candidates stop the chain comparison at that point. Self-links, cycles and paths into cycles retain their original scores. This is a bounded retrieval ordering fix, not historical/as-of retrieval or the future Historical zone.

## Batch correction (review follow-up)

Batch merges can select an uncapped predecessor score from an old-value-only sub-query. The full merged union is now ordered by replacement edges before top-k, after either best-score merge or query-rank fusion. A predecessor appearing ahead of its retrieved successor is moved immediately after it. Actual selected single-query scores remain untouched; batch order can therefore differ from descending scores. The optional `superseded_by` hit field carries the relation through both direct MCP projections and the hook/forwarder wire path.

The cycle-safe traversal is shared with the core score cap through `visitSupersession`; dangling/self/cyclic paths keep the same rules. Four batch regression tests fail on `14d8f783` and pass with the correction, including k=1, chains, score preservation and both transport projections.
